# MatchGig: Your first experience, paid

A landing page and concept for a marketplace that connects students with short, paid projects from real companies.

## The problem
You need experience to get a job, and a job to get experience. Internships are scarce, long and often unaffordable, so many students can't take the first rung of the ladder.

## The idea
MatchGig offers paid projects from one week to three months, with a clear brief, a fixed fee and a reference at the end. Companies get work done with no hiring commitment, and pay only when they approve the result.

## What the page covers
- Value proposition for students and for companies
- A "week from brief to paid" explainer (Monday brief, Friday paid)
- A sample project card (illustrative: a social media audit, 5 days, fixed fee)
- A three-step flow for companies: post a project, choose from a shortlist, approve and pay
- A waitlist sign-up that switches between "student" and "company"

## Scope and honesty
- This is a concept and landing page, not a live product. There are no real projects, companies or payments.
- The sample project is an illustration.
- The waitlist form is a front-end demo. Emails are stored only in the visitor's own browser (localStorage) and are not sent anywhere.
- Built with Claude (AI-assisted). I led the concept, positioning and product thinking.

## Tech
Single-file web page (HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript), no backend.
